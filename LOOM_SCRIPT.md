# Loom Script: AI Lead Qualification Agent (about 90 seconds)

Record in English. Screen 1080p, quiet room, close other tabs, hide email addresses and API keys.

## Before you record

- Workflow is published and all nodes are green on a test run.
- Google Sheet is open in one tab with only the header row (delete old test rows).
- Telegram (web or phone window) is visible and the chat with your bot is open.
- Gmail inbox is open in a third tab.
- Prepare the PowerShell command with the sample payload but do not run it yet.

## Shot list

**0:00-0:10 | Hook (face on camera or voice over the n8n canvas)**
"Hi, I'm [Name]. Most businesses lose leads because nobody answers fast. This is an AI agent that scores every new lead, logs it, and follows up in seconds."

**0:10-0:25 | The canvas**
Show the workflow. Point left to right: "A form hits this webhook, an AI scores the lead, it is saved to a sheet, and hot leads get a personalized email plus an instant alert to the sales team."

**0:25-0:55 | Live run**
Run the test request. Show nodes turning green in order. Open the Sheet tab: "Here is the new lead with score, label and AI summary."

**0:55-1:15 | The result**
Switch to Telegram: "Sales gets this alert immediately." Switch to Gmail: "And the lead gets a personalized follow-up, not a generic auto-reply."

**1:15-1:30 | Call to action**
"This is a demo, and I adapt it to your CRM and lead sources, like HubSpot, Slack or WhatsApp. If you want this for your business, message me on Upwork and I'll scope it for you."

## Tips

- Keep it under 2 minutes. Cut dead time while the AI step runs.
- Say "demo" clearly. Do not claim real client results.
- Put the Loom link in the Upwork portfolio item, in the GitHub README (under Work with me) and in proposals.
- Use a custom Loom thumbnail with the title "AI Lead Qualification Agent".
